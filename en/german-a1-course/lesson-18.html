07

Ich möchte einchecken

Ich möchte einchecken says what you want to do: 'I would like to check in'. Einchecken is separable, but after möchte it stays whole at the end: möchte einchecken.

08

03 · Understand the logic

Explanation

Keep the German visible while you listen to the explanation.

Explanation 1

das Gate

Wo ist das Gate? asks for the boarding gate. German adopted the English word Gate, neuter: das Gate. Many airport terms come from English, which helps the beginner.
