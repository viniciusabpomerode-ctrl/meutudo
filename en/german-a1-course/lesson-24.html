03 · Understand the logic

Explanation

Keep the German visible while you listen to the explanation.

Explanation 1

nicht

Ich verstehe nicht is the most useful phrase in the room: 'I don't understand'. nicht, no, comes at the end to negate the verb. Saying this is not shameful: that's how you learn.

Explanation 2

Wortschatz

Now let's expand vocabulary about school and the classroom.

Explanation 4

Dein Beispiel

Now try to understand instructions and ask for help, replacing information with something true about yourself.

🇩🇪 German culture

Students in Germany are encouraged to ask questions directly when they do not understand an instruction.
