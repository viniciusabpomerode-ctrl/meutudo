Explanation 1

Dialog im Kontext

Notice the pattern: the conjugated verb stays in second position and the prefix closes the sentence.

Explanation 2

dich

Ich rufe dich an comes from anrufen, to call: the an jumps to the end and the object dich, you, goes in the middle.
