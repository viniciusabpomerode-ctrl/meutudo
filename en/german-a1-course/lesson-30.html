Explanation 3

aus

seen at the beginning of the course. Now it appears within a complete conversation, not isolated: that's how you'll actually use it.

Explanation 4

einen

Ich hätte gern einen Kaffee brings back the polite request for coffee, with the accusative einen. Note how the same block works in several places: café, restaurant, hotel.
