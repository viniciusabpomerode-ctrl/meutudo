03 · Understand the logic

Explanation

Keep the German visible while you listen to the explanation.

Explanation 1

Dialog im Kontext

Notice that they follow a clear order: first the name, then the origin, and finally the city. You can use exactly this sequence when introducing yourself.

Explanation 2

heißen

Wie heißt du? is how you ask someone's name informally. The verb heißen means 'to be called'. The ß is a sharp s: say 'ráist'.

Explanation 3

Anna'

Ich heiße Anna answers 'my name is Anna'. Note that German does not use a reflexive pronoun here: there is no 'me', it's just ich heiße.

Explanation 4

woher

Woher kommst du? asks about origin: 'where are you from?'. The word woher already carries the idea of 'from where', so you don't repeat another 'from'.
