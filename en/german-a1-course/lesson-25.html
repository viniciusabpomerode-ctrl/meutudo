07

Ich habe Kopfschmerzen

Ich habe Kopfschmerzen shows how German talks about pain: uses haben, to have, plus the pain. Kopfschmerzen is composed of Kopf, head, plus Schmerzen, pains, and is plural.

08

Was fehlt Ihnen?

Was fehlt Ihnen? is the question from the clerk or doctor: literally 'what is lacking to you?', i.e., 'what do you have?'. Fehlen, to lack, with the formal dative Ihnen.

Was fehlt Ihnen?

Was fehlt Ihnen? is the question from the clerk or doctor: literally 'what is lacking to you?', i.e., 'what do you have?'. Fehlen, to lack, with the formal dative Ihnen.

Explanation 2

Seit

Seit gestern answers how long: 'since yesterday'. Seit, since, marks the start of something that continues.
