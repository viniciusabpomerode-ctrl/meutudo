03 · Understand the logic

Explanation

Keep the German visible while you listen to the explanation.

Explanation 1

Wie spät ist es?

Wie spät ist es? is the most common way to ask the time: literally 'how late is it?'. An alternative is Wie viel Uhr ist es?. Both work in everyday life.

Explanation 2

das

hours in German: means half past eight, not half past nine. German counts the half hour until nine, i.e., halfway to nine. Always think one hour ahead.

Explanation 3

wie viel Uhr?

A wie viel Uhr? asks at what time something happens. The um is the preposition for time, and the answer also comes with um: um drei Uhr. Do not confuse it with the um of numbers.

Explanation 4

Sprechen Sie nach

🇩🇪 German culture

Punctuality matters in everyday life: appointments and public transport times are usually understood as exact times.
