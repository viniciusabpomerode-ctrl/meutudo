03 · Understand the logic

Explanation

Keep the German visible while you listen to the explanation.

Explanation 1

Dialog im Kontext

Notice the difference between the name of the country and the name of the language: they are not always similar.

Explanation 2

sprechen

Ich spreche Portugiesisch shows a rule: names of languages do not take an article after sprechen and are capitalized, like every German noun.
