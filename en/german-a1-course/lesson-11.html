03 · Understand the logic

Explanation

Keep the German visible while you listen to the explanation.

Explanation 1

Was möchten Sie?

Was möchten Sie? is what the attendant asks: 'what would you like?'. Möchten is the polite form of wanting, and Sie shows that the service is formal.

Explanation 2

Sonst noch etwas?

Sonst noch etwas? is the classic counter question: 'anything else?'. It's a fixed expression; don't translate word for word. Answer with Nein, danke or order another item.
