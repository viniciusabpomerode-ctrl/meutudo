03 · Understand the logic

Explanation

Keep the German visible while you listen to the explanation.

Explanation 1

Dialog im Kontext

Note that age uses the verb to be and the phone number is said digit by digit. These are two different uses of the same numerals.

Explanation 2

Wie alt bist du?

Wie alt bist du? literally means 'how old are you?'. German uses the verb sein, to be, not haben, to have: that's why it's bist du, and the answer also uses ich bin..

Explanation 3

Vierundzwanzig

Ich bin vierundzwanzig Jahre alt shows the peculiarity of German numbers: the units come before the tens. Vierundzwanzig is 'four and twenty', i.e., twenty-four.

Explanation 4

Nummer

Wie ist deine Telefonnummer? asks for the phone number. Telefonnummer is a compound word: Telefon plus Nummer.

🇩🇪 German culture

Language and culture go hand in hand

An interesting point about Germany: Phone numbers in German are usually said digit by digit.
