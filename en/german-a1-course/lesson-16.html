03 · Understand the logic

Explanation

Keep the German visible while you listen to the explanation.

Explanation 1

Dialog im Kontext

Notice that instructions place the verb at the beginning and rely on landmarks.

Explanation 2

an der Ampel

an der Ampel indicates the landmark: 'at the traffic light'. Ampel is feminine, die Ampel, in the dative an der Ampel.

Explanation 3

Seite

auf der rechten Seite means 'on the right side'. Seite is side; the adjective recht gets the ending -en here.
