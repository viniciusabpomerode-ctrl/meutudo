03 · Understand the logic

Explanation

Keep the German visible while you listen to the explanation.

Explanation 1

Dialog im Kontext

Notice the sequence: price, size, try on and decide. It's the script for almost every purchase.

Explanation 2

Größe

means size and has the ß and ö: say 'GROY-sse'. In clothing, sizes are usually the letters S, M and L, same as international.

Explanation 3

kann

Kann ich das anprobieren? asks to try on. Anprobieren is separable, but after kann, a modal verb, it stays whole at the end, in the infinitive.
