01

Welcher Tag ist heute?

What day of the week is it today?

02

Heute ist Montag.

03

Wann hast du Geburtstag?

When is your birthday?

04

Am zwölften Mai.

On the twelfth of May.

05

Hast du am Samstag Zeit?

Do you have time on Saturday?

06

Ja, am Nachmittag.

Yes, in the afternoon.

07

der Tag.

Welcher Tag ist heute? asks for the day of the week. Welcher is 'which' in masculine, because Tag, day, is masculine: der Tag.

08

Heute ist Montag:

Heute ist Montag: the days of the week are masculine and written with a capital letter. Montag comes from Mond, moon, plus Tag: it's the 'day of the moon', like our 'Monday'.

Explanation 1

Dialog im Kontext

Note that days and months start with a capital letter and the German week starts on Monday.

Explanation 2

Tag

Heute ist Montag: the days of the week are masculine and written with a capital letter. Montag comes from Mond, moon, plus Tag: it's the 'day of the moon', like our 'Monday'.
