03 · Understand the logic

Explanation

Keep the German visible while you listen to the explanation.

Explanation 1

Dialog im Kontext

Note that when we don't understand, it's perfectly normal to ask for repetition. Spelling is a practical skill, often used on the phone.

Explanation 2

schreibt man

Wie schreibt man...? asks how something is spelled. The man is an impersonal subject, like 'a gente' or 'se' in Portuguese: schreibt man is 'is spelled'.

Explanation 3

wiederholen

wiederholen means "repeat." The approximate pronunciation is "ví-der-HÔ-len," with the main stress on HÔ. Among friends, say Kannst du das bitte wiederholen?. To speak formally, use Können Sie das bitte wiederholen?

Explanation 4

Buchstabe

Buchstabieren Sie das bitte is a formal request to spell, letter by letter. The verb buchstabieren comes from Buchstabe, which is 'letter'.

🇩🇪 German culture

Language and culture go hand in hand

It's worth knowing this cultural detail: In German, names and foreign words are often spelled out on the phone.
