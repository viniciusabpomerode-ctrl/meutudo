03 · Understand the logic

Explanation

Keep the German visible while you listen to the explanation.

Explanation 1

einen

Ich kaufe einen Apfel shows the heart of the accusative: Apfel is masculine, der Apfel, and as a direct object ein becomes einen.

Explanation 2

die

Ich nehme die Banane confirms that the feminine doesn't change: die Banane remains die in the accusative. So the accusative is less scary than it seems: only the masculine changes form.
