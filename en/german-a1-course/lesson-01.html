04 · Understand the pattern

How the conversation works

Greeting

Guten + time of day

Several German greetings use Guten followed by a time of day: Morgen, Tag or Abend.

  • Guten Morgen!in the morning
  • Guten Tag!during the day
  • Guten Abend!when greeting in the evening
Asking

Wie geht es dir?

This is an informal way to ask how someone is. Use it with friends, close colleagues and people you address with du.

Und dir?And you?
Answering

Mir geht es …

Change the ending according to how you feel. Learn the German sentence as one complete pattern.

gut · finesehr gut · very wellnicht so gut · not very well
DE
German culture note

A safe choice in formal situations

During the day, Guten Tag is a safe choice with strangers and in more formal situations.
