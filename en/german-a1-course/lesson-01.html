03 · Understand the logic

Explanation

Keep the German visible while you listen to the explanation.

Explanation 1

Guten + período do dia

In several greetings, you find Guten followed by the time of day: Morgen, Tag or Abend.

Explanation 2

Wie geht es dir?

It's an informal way to ask how someone is. Use it with friends, close colleagues and people you address as du.

Explanation 3

Mir geht es …

Complete the ending according to how you are feeling. You don't need to construct the sentence from Portuguese.
