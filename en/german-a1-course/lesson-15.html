03 · Understand the logic

Explanation

Keep the German visible while you listen to the explanation.

Explanation 1

nah

Ist das weit? means 'is it far?'. Weit means far; the opposite is nah, near. It's a very short and useful question right after asking for directions.

Explanation 2

die Bank

neben der Bank shows location: 'next to the bank'. Here Bank is feminine, die Bank, which becomes neben der Bank in the dative.

Explanation 3

Nur

nur fünf Minuten means 'only five minutes'. Nur, only or just, reassures the person asking. It's common to measure distance in minutes on foot, not in meters.

Explanation 4

Sprechen Sie nach

Now practice pronunciation: listen and repeat each sentence out loud.

🇩🇪 German culture

German cities use clear signs for streets, stations and pedestrian areas, so location words are especially useful.
