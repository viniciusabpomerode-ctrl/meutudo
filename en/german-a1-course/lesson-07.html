Was bist du von Beruf? is the standard question about profession: 'what do you do for a living?'. Beruf means profession. A common alternative is Was machst du beruflich?

08

03 · Understand the logic

Explanation

Keep the German visible while you listen to the explanation.

Explanation 1

Dialog im Kontext

Notice that no one uses an article before the profession. This is the biggest difference compared to Portuguese.

Explanation 2

Was bist du von Beruf?

Was bist du von Beruf? is the standard question about profession: 'what do you do for a living?'. Beruf means profession. A common alternative is Was machst du beruflich?

Explanation 3

Ich bin Ingenieur

Ich bin Ingenieur shows an important rule: when stating a profession, German does not use an article. It is Ich bin Ingenieur, never 'ich bin ein Ingenieur'.

Explanation 4

du: du arbeitest

Wo arbeitest du? asks for the workplace. The verb arbeiten gains an extra -e- in the form du: du arbeitest, to facilitate pronunciation after the t.
