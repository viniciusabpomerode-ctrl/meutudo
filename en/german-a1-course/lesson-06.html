03 · Understand the logic

Explanation

Keep the German visible while you listen to the explanation.

Explanation 1

Geschwister

Hast du Geschwister? asks if you have siblings in general. Geschwister already means 'brothers and sisters together' and is always plural; there is no 'a Geschwister'.

Explanation 2

wie

Wer ist das? asks 'who is this person?'. Do not confuse wer, who, with wo, where, nor with wie, how: they are three similar little words with very different meanings.

Explanation 3

Dein Beispiel
