03 · Understand the logic

Explanation

Keep the German visible while you listen to the explanation.

Explanation 1

Dein Beispiel

Now try describing the weather and choosing activities, replacing one piece of information with something true about yourself.

Explanation 2

Sonne

Es ist sonnig describes the weather with es ist plus adjective: sonnig, sunny, comes from Sonne, sun. Many weather adjectives end in -ig: windig, wolkig, neblig.

Explanation 3

Es regnet

Es regnet is a special case: the verb itself says everything, without an adjective. Regnen, to rain, always comes with the impersonal es: es regnet, es schneit, it snows.
